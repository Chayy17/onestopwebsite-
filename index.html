Frequently Asked Questions

How much does junk removal cost?

Pricing is based on how much space your items take up in our truck, plus any heavy or specialty items. The easiest way to get an exact number is to text us a photo. We'll send back an upfront price. No hidden fees, and you never pay until the job is done.

Do you offer same-day service?

Yes! When our schedule allows, we offer same-day and next-day pickups across Deer Park, Pasadena, La Porte, Baytown and Houston. Call or text early in the day for the best chance at a same-day slot.

Do you donate or recycle?

Absolutely. Usable furniture, appliances and household goods go to local charities whenever possible, and we recycle metal, cardboard and electronics. The landfill is our last stop, not our first.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. For curbside, garage or yard pickups, just leave the items out and pay by phone or online. For cleanouts inside a home, we can coordinate access with you, your realtor or your property manager (lockboxes welcome).

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, and popular payment apps. Businesses and property managers can be invoiced.
