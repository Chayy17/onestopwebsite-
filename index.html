Frequently Asked Questions

How much does junk removal cost?

Pricing is based on how much space your items take up in our truck, plus any heavy or specialty items. Text us a photo for a free ballpark range, then we give you a firm price in person before any work starts. No hidden fees, and you never pay until the job is done.

Is the on-site estimate really free?

Yes. The estimate is free with no obligation. We come out, look at everything and give you an exact price. If you say yes, we can usually haul it the same visit. If not, you don't pay a thing.

Do you offer same-day junk removal?

Yes. When our schedule allows, we offer same-day and next-day pickups across Deer Park, Pasadena, La Porte, Baytown and Houston. Call or text early in the day for the best chance at a same-day slot.

Do you donate or recycle items?

Yes. Usable furniture, appliances and household goods go to local charities whenever possible, and we recycle metal, cardboard and electronics. The landfill is our last stop, not our first.

Do I need to be home?

No. For curbside, garage or yard pickups, just leave the items out and pay by phone or online. For cleanouts inside a home, we can coordinate access with you, your property manager or your contractor.
